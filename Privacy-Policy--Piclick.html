Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Piclick app (also referred to as Piclick Fast Clean) operated by Zhelei Fan (the “Service Provider”).

Piclick is designed as an on-device photo management app. It does not operate its own servers that collect your photos, and it does not sell or share your personal information for advertising.

Information Piclick Uses on Your Device

To provide its features, Piclick accesses data that already exists on your device, with the permissions you grant in iOS:

  • Photo library: photos and videos, including metadata such as creation date and, where present, the location already saved in a photo, so Piclick can show, group, analyze, and help you delete items.
  • Location permission: used only to reverse-geocode shooting coordinates already stored in photos into city names. Piclick does not determine or track your current location.
  • Face ID / device passcode: used only to unlock the in-app private vault on this device.

Photo analysis (for example Smart Categories, duplicates, and blur detection) runs locally on the device. Scan results, language preference, and similar app state are stored on the device. A trial-period timestamp is stored in the device Keychain so it can survive reinstalls.

Information Sent Off the Device

Piclick does not include analytics, advertising, cookies, pixels, or other tracking SDKs, and it does not collect your IP address, screen-by-screen usage, or time spent in the app.

Limited information may leave the device only in these cases:

  • Apple reverse geocoding: city grouping may send a photo’s saved coordinates to Apple’s geocoding service (or the regional provider Apple uses) to obtain a place name. Coordinates are not sent to the Service Provider.
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit: purchases, restores, and subscription status are processed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy. Piclick does not receive your full payment card details.
  • iCloud Photos: if a photo is stored in iCloud, Apple’s PhotoKit may download it to the device so Piclick can display or analyze it. That transfer is between your device and Apple, not to the Service Provider.
  • Optional email: if you use Feedback or otherwise email the Service Provider, the content of that message is sent through your mail app to piclickappadm@gmail.com .

What the Service Provider Does Not Do

  • Does not sell personal information.
  • Does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Does not require you to create an account or provide an email address to use Piclick.
  • Does not send marketing communications through the app.

Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

The Service Provider does not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by CCPA/CPRA. Because Piclick does not maintain a cloud account of your photos, deletion of library content is done on your device (for example by deleting photos or uninstalling the app). For questions or requests, contact the Service Provider at the email below.

Children

The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age, or such higher age as required by applicable law. The Service Provider does not knowingly solicit data from children or market the Application to them.

If you have reason to believe that a child has provided personally identifiable information to the Service Provider by email, please contact the Service Provider so that the necessary actions can be taken.

Changes

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time by posting the updated policy in the app with an effective date. Where required by law, consent will be sought for material changes.

Your Consent

Where processing is based on consent, you provide that consent by granting the relevant iOS permission or by affirmatively using the relevant feature. You may withdraw photo, location, or Face ID access at any time in iOS Settings. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out on the device.
